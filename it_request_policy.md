
# Northstar Systems — IT Request Management Policy

Policy ID: IT-POL-042
Version: 1.0
Classification: Fictional training document

## 1. Software Access

Power BI Desktop access requires:
- Requester's manager approval
- Data Governance Team approval
- A documented business justification

The target processing time is two business days after
all required approvals are received.

Salesforce access requires:
- Requester's manager approval
- Salesforce System Owner approval
- Business justification and required access role

## 2. IT Equipment

Keyboard, mouse, and monitor requests require
the requester's manager to approve the equipment.

Standard IT equipment has a target fulfillment time
of four business days after approval and stock confirmation.

Critical-priority IT equipment requires approval from:
- Requester's manager
- IT Service Lead

Critical requests receive an initial assessment within
one business day. Fulfillment timing is not guaranteed.

## 3. Priority Levels

Low: Nonurgent requests with minimal business impact.

Medium: Standard requests for routine business operations.

High: Requests with significant business impact or
an approaching work deadline.

Critical: Requests with major operational disruption.

Critical priority does not guarantee immediate fulfillment.

## 4. Request Lifecycle

Submitted: Request recorded and awaiting further processing.

In Review: The request is being assessed.

Approved: Required approval has been obtained.

In Progress: The request is being fulfilled.

Completed: The request is fulfilled and closed.

Rejected: The request was rejected and closed.

Cancelled: The request was cancelled and closed.

## 5. Modification and Cancellation

Active requests may be modified according to the
request-management application's supported fields.

Completed, Rejected, and Cancelled requests cannot be
modified or cancelled through the normal process.

Cancelling an active request requires explicit confirmation.

## 6. Duplicate Prevention

A requester must not create multiple active requests
for the same resource.

An existing active request should be reviewed or modified
rather than duplicated.

## 7. Knowledge Limitations

This document does not specify VPN password rotation rules.

Policy targets are not guaranteed processing times.

Approval decisions must be confirmed by authorized reviewers.

The actual status of a request must be retrieved from
the request-management database, not inferred from this policy.
